# NimbusCart Cancellation Policy

**Document ID:** POL-CAN-001
**Last updated:** 05 January 2026
**Owner:** Order Operations Team

## When an order can be cancelled

Cancellation depends entirely on the current status of the order:

| Order status | Can it be cancelled? | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Processing | Yes, free of charge | Order stopped, full refund in 3-5 business days |
| Shipped | No | Must be refused at the door or returned after delivery |
| Out for Delivery | No | Must be refused at the door or returned after delivery |
| Delivered | No | Use the Return Policy instead |
| Delayed | Yes, free of charge | Treated the same as Processing |
| Cancelled | Already cancelled | No action needed |

The practical rule: **an order can be cancelled for free at any point before the
carrier collects it.** Once the status changes to Shipped, cancellation is no
longer possible and the order must go through the returns process instead.

## How to cancel

Provide your order ID to support and request cancellation. The system checks the
live order status before confirming. If the status has already moved to Shipped
between the customer's request and the check, the cancellation is refused and the
customer is offered the returns route instead.

## Cancellation refunds

Cancellation refunds are faster than return refunds because there is no physical
inspection step:

- **Card and wallet payments:** 3 to 5 business days
- **Bank transfer:** 5 to 7 business days
- **NimbusCart store credit:** immediate

## Partial cancellations

If an order contains several items, individual lines can be cancelled while the
order is still in Processing. Cancelling part of an order may push the remaining
order value below the $50 free-shipping threshold, in which case the $4.99
standard shipping fee is applied to the remaining items.

## Cancellation by NimbusCart

NimbusCart may cancel an order without customer request when:

- The item is out of stock after payment was taken
- The delivery address is undeliverable or on our excluded list
- The payment is flagged by our fraud screening system
- A pricing error was published on the product page

In all these cases the customer receives a full refund, including shipping, and
an email explaining the reason. Fraud-flag cancellations are always reviewed by a
human specialist and cannot be discussed by automated support.

## Subscription cancellations

NimbusCart Plus membership can be cancelled at any time from the account
settings page. Cancellation stops the next renewal; the current paid period runs
to its end date. Membership fees already charged are not pro-rated or refunded.
