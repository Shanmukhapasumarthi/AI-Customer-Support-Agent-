# NimbusCart Frequently Asked Questions

**Document ID:** DOC-FAQ-001
**Last updated:** 10 April 2026
**Owner:** Customer Experience Team

## Orders

**How do I find my order ID?**
Your order ID is in the confirmation email sent immediately after checkout, and
in the Orders section of your account. It always looks like ORD followed by four
digits, for example ORD1001.

**Can I change the items in my order after placing it?**
No. Items cannot be added or swapped after checkout. Cancel the order while it is
still in Processing and place a new one.

**Can I change my delivery address?**
Only while the order status is Processing. Once shipped, the address is locked
with the carrier.

**Do you offer gift wrapping?**
Yes, $3.99 per item, selectable at checkout. Gift-wrapped orders do not show
prices on the packing slip.

## Delivery

**How long does shipping take?**
Standard Shipping is 5-7 business days, Express is 2-3 business days, and
Priority Overnight arrives the next business day. Add 1-2 business days of
processing time before dispatch.

**Is shipping free?**
Standard Shipping is free on orders over $50. Below that it costs $4.99.

**Do you ship internationally?**
Yes, to 34 countries. International Standard takes 10-18 business days. Import
duties are paid by the recipient.

**My tracking number is not working.**
Tracking can take up to 24 hours to activate after the parcel is scanned. If it
is still inactive after 24 hours, contact support with your order ID.

## Returns and refunds

**What is the return window?**
30 calendar days from delivery.

**Can I return an item after 15 days?**
Yes. The deadline is 30 days, so day 15 is well within the window. The item is
inspected more strictly after day 15 and must show no signs of use.

**How long do refunds take?**
2 business days for inspection after the item arrives, then 5-7 business days for
the money to reach the original payment method.

**Can I get a refund to a different card?**
No. Refunds always go back to the original payment method. If that card is closed,
a human agent arranges a bank transfer after identity verification.

**Do you do exchanges?**
No. Return the item for a refund and place a new order.

## Account

**How do I reset my password?**
Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for
60 minutes.

**How do I delete my account?**
Email a deletion request from your registered address. Deletion is permanent and
processed within 30 days. Orders in progress must be completed or cancelled first.

**What is NimbusCart Plus?**
A membership costing $49 per year that includes free Express Shipping on all
orders, early access to sales, and an extended 45-day return window.

## Products and warranty

**What is the warranty period?**
12 months as standard. Home Appliances and Furniture get 24 months, Accessories 6
months, Apparel 3 months. Consumables have no warranty.

**Does the warranty cover accidental damage?**
No. Drops, liquid damage, and cosmetic wear are excluded. NimbusCare Plus adds one
accidental damage claim per year.

**Are your products covered by a manufacturer warranty as well?**
NimbusCart handles all warranty claims directly. You do not need to contact the
manufacturer.

## Support

**What are your support hours?**
The AI assistant is available 24 hours a day. Human specialists are available
Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern Time, and Saturday 10:00 AM to 4:00
PM Eastern Time.

**How do I reach a human agent?**
Ask the assistant to escalate, or reply to any order email. Complaints, payment
disputes, refund disputes, damaged deliveries, and lost shipments are routed to a
human automatically.

**How quickly does a human agent respond?**
Within 1 business day for standard cases and within 4 hours for payment disputes
and damaged deliveries.
