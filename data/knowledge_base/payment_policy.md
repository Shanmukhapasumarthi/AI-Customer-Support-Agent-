# NimbusCart Payment Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PAY-001
**Last updated:** 30 January 2026
**Owner:** Finance and Risk Team

## Accepted payment methods

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- NimbusPay wallet
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- NimbusCart gift cards and store credit
- Bank transfer (orders above $500 only)
- Buy Now Pay Later through our partner SplitPay (orders $50 - $2000)

## When you are charged

For card payments an **authorisation hold** is placed when the order is
submitted, and the amount is **captured when the order ships**. This means the
money leaves the account at dispatch, not at checkout.

An authorisation hold that is never captured, for example on a cancelled order,
is released by the customer's bank within **5 to 10 business days**. NimbusCart
cannot release a hold faster; this timing is controlled by the issuing bank.

## Duplicate or double charges

A pending authorisation and a captured charge for the same order can appear as
two separate lines on a bank statement. In almost all cases the pending line
disappears on its own within 5 to 10 business days.

If two identical **captured** charges appear for the same order ID, that is a
genuine duplicate charge. Duplicate charge cases involve moving money and are
always escalated to a **human specialist in the Finance team**. Automated support
cannot issue, reverse, or confirm a payment reversal.

## Failed payments

Common reasons a payment fails:

- Insufficient funds
- Card expired or details entered incorrectly
- Billing address does not match the address on file with the bank
- Bank declined the transaction as a suspected fraudulent purchase
- Daily transaction limit exceeded

When a payment fails the order is held for **48 hours** so the customer can retry
with another method. After 48 hours the order is cancelled automatically and any
reserved stock is released.

## Currency and taxes

All prices are shown in US dollars. International customers are charged in USD
and their bank applies its own conversion rate and any foreign transaction fee.
NimbusCart does not control these fees.

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address. Import duties
and customs charges for international orders are the responsibility of the
recipient and are not included in the order total.

## Payment disputes and chargebacks

If a customer raises a chargeback with their bank, the order is frozen and all
communication moves to our Finance team. Automated support must not discuss,
negotiate, or predict the outcome of a chargeback. These cases require a human
specialist.

## Invoices

A tax invoice is emailed automatically when an order ships and is also available
in the account order history. Invoice reissues for a different billing entity
require verification by a human agent.

## Security

NimbusCart never stores full card numbers. Payments are processed by a PCI-DSS
Level 1 certified provider. NimbusCart staff and automated support will **never**
ask for a full card number, CVV, PIN, or account password. Any message requesting
these is fraudulent and should be reported.
