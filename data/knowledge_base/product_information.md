### AuraSound Mini (PROD002) - True Wireless Earbuds

Compact earbuds for exercise and travel.

- Active noise cancellation: single-microphone, up to 22 dB reduction
- Battery life: 7 hours per bud, 28 hours total with the charging case
- Charging: USB-C and wireless charging
- Weight: 4.8 g per bud
- Water resistance: IPX5, sweat and light rain resistant
- Warranty: 12 months

### Difference between AuraSound X1 and AuraSound Mini

Customers frequently ask how these two compare. The short answer:

| Feature | AuraSound X1 | AuraSound Mini |
|---|---|---|
| Form factor | Over-ear headphones | In-ear earbuds |
| Noise cancellation | Hybrid, up to 32 dB | Single-mic, up to 22 dB |
| Battery (single charge) | 40 hours | 7 hours (28 h with case) |
| Water resistance | None | IPX5 |
| Best for | Flights, long focus sessions, best sound quality | Running, gym, commuting light |
| Portability | Folds, but bulky | Pocket-sized |

Choose the **X1** for maximum noise cancellation, battery life, and sound
quality. Choose the **Mini** for portability and workouts. The X1 is not sweat
resistant and is not recommended for exercise.
