# NimbusCart Refund Policy

**Document ID:** POL-REF-001
**Last updated:** 12 March 2026
**Owner:** Customer Experience Team

## Refund eligibility window

NimbusCart provides refunds within **30 calendar days of delivery**. The 30-day
window starts on the date the carrier marks the order as Delivered, not on the
date the order was placed.

Items must be:

- Unused and in resalable condition
- Returned in the original packaging, including all accessories and manuals
- Accompanied by the order ID or the original invoice

## Refund processing time

Once a returned item arrives at our warehouse, our inspection team reviews it
within **2 business days**. After the inspection is approved, the refund is
issued to the original payment method within **5 to 7 business days**.

Total expected time from the moment you post the return: approximately
**8 to 12 business days**.

## Refund amounts

- **Full refund:** Item returned within 30 days, unused, original packaging intact.
- **Partial refund (80% of item price):** Item returned within 30 days but with
  damaged packaging or missing accessories. The 20% deduction is a restocking fee.
- **No refund:** Item returned after the 30-day window, or an item listed as
  non-refundable below.

Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is caused by a
NimbusCart error, such as a wrong item shipped or a manufacturing defect.

## Non-refundable items

The following categories cannot be refunded under any circumstances:

- Gift cards and digital vouchers
- Downloadable software and digital licences
- Perishable goods
- Personal hygiene and intimate apparel items once the seal is broken
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale"

## Refund method

Refunds are always returned to the **original payment method**. We cannot refund
to a different card, bank account, or wallet. If the original card has expired or
been cancelled, contact support and a human agent will arrange a bank transfer.
This process requires identity verification and takes an additional 3 business days.

## Refund disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, a refund amount, or believe a refund was
not received after 12 business days, the case must be escalated to a **human
support specialist**. Automated support cannot override a refund decision, adjust
a refund amount, or reverse a restocking fee.
