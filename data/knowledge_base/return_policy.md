# NimbusCart Return Policy

**Document ID:** POL-RET-001
**Last updated:** 12 March 2026
**Owner:** Customer Experience Team

## Return window

Items may be returned within **30 calendar days of delivery**. This is the same
window as the refund policy: a return is the physical act of sending the item
back, and a refund is the money movement that follows a successful return.

Returns requested **after 15 days but within 30 days** are still accepted, but the
item is inspected more strictly and must show no signs of use. A common customer
question is whether an item can be returned after 15 days - the answer is yes,
the deadline is 30 days, not 15.

Returns requested **after 30 days** are refused automatically and the item is
shipped back to the customer at their expense.

## How to start a return

1. Locate your order ID (format: ORD followed by four digits, e.g. ORD1001).
2. Request a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number from support.
3. Pack the item in its original packaging with all accessories.
4. Write the RMA number clearly on the outside of the parcel.
5. Drop the parcel at any partner carrier location within 7 days of receiving
   the RMA. RMA numbers expire after 7 days.

Parcels received without a valid RMA number cannot be matched to an order and
will be held for 30 days before being disposed of.

## Return shipping costs

- **NimbusCart pays** when the return is our fault: wrong item shipped, item
  faulty on arrival, or item not as described.
- **Customer pays** when the return is a change of mind, wrong size ordered, or
  the customer no longer wants the item. A prepaid label can be issued and the
  cost ($6.99 domestic) is deducted from the refund.

## Exchanges

NimbusCart does not process direct exchanges. To swap an item for a different
size, colour, or model, return the original item for a refund and place a new
order. This keeps the two transactions independent and avoids stock holds.

## Condition requirements

Returned items are inspected against these criteria:

- No scratches, stains, odours, or signs of wear
- All original tags still attached
- All accessories, cables, manuals, and free gifts included
- Original manufacturer packaging undamaged

Failing any criterion results in a partial refund with a 20% restocking fee, as
described in the Refund Policy.

## Items that cannot be returned

Gift cards, digital downloads, perishable goods, opened hygiene products, and
Final Sale clearance items cannot be returned. This list matches the
non-refundable list in the Refund Policy.
