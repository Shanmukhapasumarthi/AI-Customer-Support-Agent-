# NimbusCart Shipping Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SHP-001
**Last updated:** 28 February 2026
**Owner:** Logistics Team

## Shipping options and delivery times

All delivery estimates are counted in **business days** and begin after the order
has been dispatched, not from when the order was placed.

| Shipping method | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Shipping | 5 - 7 business days | Free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99 |
| Express Shipping | 2 - 3 business days | $12.99 |
| Priority Overnight | Next business day | $24.99 |
| International Standard | 10 - 18 business days | Calculated at checkout |

## Order processing time

Orders are picked and packed within **1 to 2 business days** of being placed.
Orders placed after 2:00 PM local warehouse time are processed the next business
day. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays begin processing on the next
business day.

So the total time a customer waits is: processing time (1-2 days) plus the
delivery time for the chosen shipping method.

## Order tracking

A tracking number is emailed as soon as the carrier scans the parcel. Tracking
information can take up to 24 hours to become active on the carrier website.

Customers can check the live status of any order by providing their order ID to
support. Order statuses used by NimbusCart are:

- **Processing** - payment confirmed, item being picked in the warehouse
- **Shipped** - handed to the carrier and in transit
- **Out for Delivery** - on the delivery vehicle, arriving today
- **Delivered** - successfully delivered
- **Delayed** - held up by weather, customs, or a carrier issue
- **Cancelled** - order stopped before dispatch
- **Returned** - item sent back to our warehouse

## Shipping destinations

NimbusCart ships to 34 countries. We do **not** ship to PO boxes, military APO
or FPO addresses, or freight forwarding warehouses.

## Delayed and lost shipments

A domestic shipment is considered **delayed** if it has not arrived 3 business
days past its expected delivery date. It is considered **lost** after 10 business
days past the expected delivery date.

For a lost shipment, customers may choose a free replacement or a full refund.
Claims for lost shipments must be raised within 30 days of the expected delivery
date and require review by a human support specialist.

## Damaged deliveries

If a parcel arrives visibly damaged, take photographs of the packaging and the
item before unpacking further, and report it within **48 hours of delivery**.
Damaged-on-arrival cases are handled as a priority replacement at no cost.
Because these cases involve a claim against the carrier, they are always routed
to a human support specialist.

## Address changes

A delivery address can only be changed while the order status is **Processing**.
Once the order is Shipped the address is locked with the carrier and cannot be
changed by NimbusCart.
