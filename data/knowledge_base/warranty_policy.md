# NimbusCart Warranty Policy

**Document ID:** POL-WAR-001
**Last updated:** 19 February 2026
**Owner:** After-Sales Support Team

## Standard warranty period

Every product sold by NimbusCart carries a **12-month limited warranty** from the
date of delivery, unless a different period is stated on the product page.

Category-specific warranty periods:

| Product category | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Electronics | 12 months |
| Audio | 12 months |
| Home Appliances | 24 months |
| Furniture | 24 months |
| Accessories | 6 months |
| Apparel | 3 months (manufacturing defects only) |
| Consumables and batteries | No warranty |

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers **manufacturing defects and component failures** that occur
under normal use, including:

- Device not powering on
- Buttons, ports, switches, or hinges failing
- Battery holding less than 60% of rated capacity within the warranty period
- Screen or display defects such as dead pixels or backlight failure
- Seams, zips, or stitching failing on apparel

## What the warranty does not cover

- Accidental damage, drops, crushing, or impact
- Liquid or water damage on products not rated water-resistant
- Normal cosmetic wear such as scratches and scuffs
- Damage caused by unauthorised repair or modification
- Damage from using non-approved chargers or accessories
- Consumable parts such as filters, batteries sold separately, and printer ink
- Loss or theft of the product

## Extended warranty

**NimbusCare Plus** can be purchased within 30 days of delivery. It extends the
standard warranty by an additional 24 months and adds one accidental damage claim
per year. Pricing is 12% of the item purchase price. NimbusCare Plus is
non-transferable if the item is resold.

## Making a warranty claim

1. Provide your order ID and a description of the fault.
2. Supply photographs or a short video showing the defect.
3. Our technical team responds with an assessment within 3 business days.
4. If approved, we repair, replace, or refund at our discretion, in that order of
   preference.

Warranty repairs are completed within 10 to 15 business days of the item reaching
our service centre. Shipping to the service centre is free for approved claims.

## Warranty versus refund

A warranty claim and a refund are different processes. A refund is available for
30 days after delivery for any reason. A warranty claim is available for the full
warranty period, but only for covered defects, and the outcome is usually a
repair or replacement rather than money back.

If a covered defect cannot be repaired or replaced, a pro-rated refund is issued
based on the remaining warranty period.
